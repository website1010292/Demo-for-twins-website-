Our Story

The Art of Pakistani Dressing

Twin Design was built around a simple idea: that everyday Pakistani clothing deserves the same care as occasion wear. We work closely with our fabric sources to select pure Boski and cotton that hold their shape and color, then finish each suit with clean stitching and considered detail.

Our approach stays close to tradition — Boski, lawn, and cotton in their honest forms — while the silhouettes and styling are built for how our customers actually dress today, at home and abroad.
